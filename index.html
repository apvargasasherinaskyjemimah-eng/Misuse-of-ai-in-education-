Introduction

Artificial intelligence is growing in education, but are students using it to learn or simply to avoid learing?

Imagine a student who struggles with a lesson. Instead of trying to understand it, they turn to AI for answers. Eventually, they become more focused on getting answers than actually learning. This website discusses the misuse of artificial intelligence in education, its negative effects, and why students should learn to use it responsibly.

Step 1: The 5 Whys

Chosen Problem: Misuse of AI in Education

Why 1: Why do students misuse AI in education?

Because they rely completely on artificial intelligence to complete their academic responsibilities, such as assignments, projects, and essays, instead of doing the work with their own knowledge and understandng the lessons. This causes them to be dependent on AI rather than actually learning and developing their own skills and abilities.

Why 2: Why do students rely completely on AI?

Because they find AI more convenient, fast, and easy to use especially when completing schoolworks. It provides instant answers, solutions, and explanations, allowing them to finish their tasks faster, and with less effort. As a result, they become more dependent on AI whenever they face difficult academic activities.

Why 3: Why do students prefer using AI instead of learning dependently?

Because they want to avoid difficult tasks, save time, reduce their activities, and achieve good grades without putting in much effort. Some students also struggle to understand their lessons and activities, making AI an easier option than studying and solving problems on their own.

Why 4: Why do students fail to recognize the risks of completely relying on AI?

Because they lack sufficient awareness of the negative effects of misusing artificial intelligence in their education. They may not understand that submitting AI generated work as their own can be considered as academic dishonesty and that too much dependence on AI can affect their critical thinking, creativity, and independent learning skills.

Why 5: Why do students lack awareness about the responsible use of AI?

Because they do not recieve enough guidance, knowledge, and clear instructions from schools and teachers about the proper use of artificial intelligence. without proper guidance and clear school policies, students may become too dependent on AI and use it to complete their schoolworks instead of developing their knowledge and skills and understanding their lessons.

Root Cause

The main root cause of the misuse of AI in education is the lack of proper guidance, awareness, and clear school policies and rules regarding the responsible use of artificial intelligence. Because students are not given enough information about its proper use, they may become overly dependent on AI to complete their academic responsibilities instead of using their own knowldege and critical thinking abilities.

Step 2: Investigate Historical Impact

Education Before Generative AI

Before generative AI became widely available, students mainly relied on textbooks, libraries, teachers, and their own knowledge to complete schooolworks. They researched information manually, wrote their own essays, solved problems independently, and asked teachers and classmates for help when needed. Teachers guided students by explaning lessons, giving instructions, and checking their work. Even tho computers and the internet were already used in education students still had to spend time reading, researching, and understanding lessons on their own. Overall, education before generative AI encouraged independent learning, critical thinking, and creativity.

Education After the Rise of Generative AI

After generative AI became widely available, education changed significantly as students gained access to tools that could quickly provide answers, explain lessons, summarize informations, generate essays. Students can now use AI to help with assignments, research, and understanding difficult topics. Teachers can also use AI to prepare lessons and create learning materials. However, while AI make learning faster, and more convenient, some students have overly become to dependent on it and use it to complete their schoolworks instead of thinking and learning independently. This can affect their critical thinking, creativity, problem solving skills. and academic honesty. Overall, generative AI has made education more accessible and convenient, but it also requires students to use it responsibly.

Historical Comparison

Before Generative AI, students mainly relied on textbooks, libraries, teachers, and independent research to complete their schoolwork. They spent more time gathering information, writing assignments, and solving problems on their own. After Generative AI became widely available, students gained access to tools that could quickly generate answers, essays, and explanations. Although learning became faster and more convenient, some students also became overly dependent on AI. This shows how education has shifted from traditional, independent learning to technology-assisted learning, making responsible AI use increasingly important.

Step 3: Compare with Present-Day Impact

People in the Philippines

Today, Generative AI has become increasingly popular among Filipino students and teachers. Many students use AI tools like ChatGPT to research information, understand difficult lessons, answer questions, summarize topics, and complete school assignments. It has made learning more convenient by providing quick explanations and helping students with tasks they find challenging. Teachers can also use AI to prepare lesson plans, create learning materials, and find new ways to explain topics. As a result, AI has become a useful tool in many educational activities across the Philippines.

However, the growing use of Generative AI has also brought challenges to Philippine education. Some students rely too much on AI to complete their assignments instead of using their own knowledge and critical-thinking skills. This can lead to plagiarism, cheating, spreading false information, and reduced creativity and independent learning. There are also concerns about students who have limited access to reliable internet connections and digital devices. Although AI offers many opportunities to improve education, students and teachers must learn to use it responsibly to ensure that it supports learning rather than replaces it.

Other Countries

Today, Generative AI is widely used in education in many countries, including the United States, Singapore, Japan, South Korea, and several European countries. Students use AI tools like ChatGPT to research topics, answer questions, summarize lessons, and improve their schoolwork. Teachers also use AI to create lesson plans, prepare learning materials, and provide support to students. According to the OECD, around 75% of teachers in Singapore and the United Arab Emirates reported using AI in their work, showing how AI has become part of modern education in some countries.

However, other countries also face challenges caused by the misuse of AI. Some students depend too much on AI to complete assignments, which can affect their independent thinking, creativity, and problem-solving skills. There are also concerns about cheating, plagiarism, inaccurate information, and student privacy. Different countries are developing rules and guidelines to encourage responsible AI use in schools. Although Generative AI offers many benefits, it is important for students and teachers worldwide to use it as a learning tool rather than a replacement for actual learning.

Communities Today

Today, Generative AI is influencing communities around the world by changing how people learn, communicate, work, and access information. Students use AI for schoolwork, businesses use it to improve their services, and workers use it to complete tasks more efficiently. It also helps people create content, translate languages, and find information more easily. As a result, AI has become an increasingly important part of everyday life in many communities. However, the growing use of AI also brings challenges. Some people worry about job opportunities, misinformation, privacy, and the spread of AI-generated fake images and videos. Communities with limited internet access and technology may also struggle to benefit from these advancements. According to the World Bank, unequal access to digital infrastructure and AI technology can widen the gap between communities. This shows the importance of responsible AI use, digital education, and equal access to technology.

Possible Solutions

  • Teach students how to use AI responsibly.
  • Encourage students to understand answers instead of copying them directly.
  • Check AI-generated information using reliable sources.
  • Promote academic honesty and independent thinking.
  • Provide teachers with training on AI literacy.
  • Establish clear classroom guidelines for AI use.
